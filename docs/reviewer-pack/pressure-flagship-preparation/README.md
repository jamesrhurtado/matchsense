# Pressure flagship — unassigned review preparation

No reviewer has been recruited or rated this packet. The CSV intentionally has
headers only. Use anonymous participant IDs only after consent; keep identities,
contact details and recordings privately outside the repository. Do not send or
publish this packet without the owner's direction.

**Stage 1, independent football/source review:** read `match.json` and decompress
`source-through-92s.jsonl.gz`. It contains only observations available through
92 seconds; the manifest freezes its hash. Independently integrate opponents
within 6 m around blue-11 for [85,87) s and blue-09 for [88.3,90.3) s. Use left
observations with endpoint positions, gaps ≤200 ms and explicit actor/team
control. Record covered seconds and time-weighted means. Identify the prior
three completed team passes within 120 s before release; confirm the direction
and distinction between forward pass displacement and subsequent retention.
Derive these before opening the candidate. Missing observations are not zero
opponents. Assess whether the thresholds are useful rather than treating the
engineering definition as proof of football significance.

**Stage 2, wording and usefulness:** reveal `stage-2-candidate.json`. Compare its
simple/analyst text in English and Spanish and passer/receiver focus against the
independently derived facts. Check whether a first-time viewer understands that
fractional opponents are averages, not partial players or probabilities. Flag
unsupported intent, difficulty, line-breaking, causal or goal-creation wording.
A Spanish reviewer should judge fluency and clarity independently.

**Interactive first-time viewer task:** use a disposable flagship session at 92 s,
approve the exact current candidate, and open the companion. Ask the participant
to explain the values, locate interval/coverage evidence, switch to the relevant
receiver, and recover the moment through recap. Do not teach the answers during
the attempt. Record assistance/confusion and voluntary comments separately from
any rating. Never treat successful automated browser checks as participant data.

Rating anchors: usefulness/clarity 1 = not useful/unclear, 3 = mixed, 5 = useful/
clear. Leave unanswered items blank; do not turn missing ratings into zeros.
For validity fields record yes/no/unsure and a note, not a fabricated percentage.
The [pilot protocol](../../match-coverage-pilot.md) governs consent and findings;
this source scene is separately versioned from its original mixed-event key.
