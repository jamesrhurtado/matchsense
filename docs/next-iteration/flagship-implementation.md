# Flagship iteration — implementation contract

24 October 2026. Authorized by the owner after the original-plan review.

Choose **Pressure Swing** as the flagship, defined as a nearby-opponent proxy,
rather than an uncalibrated Chaos Index, pass-difficulty score or tactical cause.
The equal-weight rubric rewards a working, understandable, evidenced pipeline;
metric names or extra agent roles alone do not establish innovation.

## Frozen nearby-opponent definition

For an already eligible, explicitly received pass, compare time-weighted mean
opponents within 6 m of the passer during [release−2 s, release), with mean
opponents within 6 m of its observed receiver during [reception, reception+2 s).
The before window requires observed passer control, the after window observed
receiver control. Require complete identity/coordinate coverage per accepted
interval, gaps ≤200 ms, ≥95% covered time in each 2 s window, mean-before ≥1,
a decrease ≥1 nearby opponent, and observed forward pass displacement ≥10 m.
These are provisional presentation gates, not validated football significance.
A nonqualifying/missing window produces no Pressure Swing insight. Units are
average nearby opponents, not probability, difficulty, pressing intent or xG.

Retain the existing 3 s team-retention confirmation and freeze source readiness.
Compare preceding three completed team passes only under the existing two-minute
population contract. Any comparison is explicitly scoped to those passes in this
replay. A source revision invalidates only affected exact approvals. No inferred
assist, defensive line, deliberate trap, or pass-caused-goal wording is permitted.

## Deliverables

- Additive v6 pressure packages; v1–v5 bytes/hashes/readers remain valid.
- Additive flagship discovery/correction fixture IDs ending 5/6, with pass
  histories inside the comparison window, coherent motion and all required old
  event families retained. Old fixtures remain unchanged.
- Pressure family through evidence, Director/Reviewer workflow, protected review,
  bilingual role-aware variants, viewer timeline/recap and mixed selection.
- Human-readable before/action/after evidence; technical audit remains available.
- Separate supported relevance (“Why this moment?”) from “Check the evidence”.
- Casual, analyst and player-role presentations over the same approved facts.
- Bounded overlay hold to reduce interruptions, with goals/manual selection and
  source correction overriding it. Scope new policy to new flagship sessions.
- Local deterministic mode, independent reference/negative/correction cases,
  real browser/mobile checks and version-specific release/pilot/demo materials.
- Assess/verify existing-model behavior without new resources or extra roles;
  report unavailable access or failures accurately. No external publication or
  deployment is authorized here. Recruitment and findings remain human work.
