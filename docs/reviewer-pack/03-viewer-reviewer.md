# WHY NOW viewer testing manual

## Your assignment

Help us find out whether football explanations are understandable and whether you can use the viewer experience without coaching. We want to see what makes sense to you and what does not.

Session length: 30 to 40 minutes. Instrument: R1, 22 October 2026.

## Who this test is for

You do not need to be an expert. Occasional football viewers and regular fans are both useful participants. No experience with broadcasting, AI or software testing is needed. Tell us how often you watch football so we can understand your perspective.

## What you need to know

WHY/NOW presents football explanations, evidence and ways to explore approved moments. The session uses a synthetic replay with fictional teams and players. We are testing the product, not your football knowledge or ability to use a computer.

You do not have to like it. Confusion, disagreement and uncertainty are valuable findings. Please do not watch a product tutorial, read other reviewers' responses or discuss your answers before your session.

## Preparation and participation

- Use your usual phone or laptop, as agreed with the facilitator. Bring glasses or other accessibility tools you normally use. Tell us if text or controls are difficult to see.
- You will receive a viewer link. No Azure account or AI subscription is needed. Do not share the session link publicly.
- Read the task, try it independently, and say when you have finished or cannot continue. Work without explaining your reasoning aloud during any timed task; we will talk afterward.
- Asking for help is allowed. The facilitator may first ask what you expected to happen.
- You can pause, skip a question or stop. Recording and public quotation require separate permission.

## Your tasks

The facilitator will give you one task at a time. Please do not practice later tasks in advance. There may be several ways to complete a task.

### V1 Understand the moment

Read the current explanation. In your own words, tell us what happened and what changed. Identify who did what, if the explanation tells you. Say what you are uncertain about.

### V2 Check a statement

Choose one statement in the explanation and find information that supports it. Explain what that information tells you. Is there anything you still cannot conclude?

### V3 Choose how you want to view it

Find a more detailed explanation and then focus on the player assigned by the facilitator. Explain what changed. If you can comfortably read a second available language, try that too; otherwise skip the language change.

### V4 Visit an earlier moment

Find the earlier approved moment assigned by the facilitator and explore it. Explain which moment you are viewing and whether you think the shared current story has changed. Then return to the current moment.

### V5 Catch up

Imagine you missed the last part of this replay. Find a summary of what has been approved so far. Explain one development and open the supporting moment. If similar moments are grouped, explain what you think the group represents.

### V6 Notice a publication change

The facilitator will make one publication change in this test session. Look again at the available stories and summary. Describe what changed, what remains and whether anything is confusing.
