# Moving matchday demo and guided workflow

Owner feedback on 24 October 2026 exposed three presentation failures: the
synthetic off-ball grid barely moved, the raw position-sample count looked like
an incident count, and “Show why” opened evidence below the fold without taking
the user there. This is product-owner feedback, not a recruited user study.

## Delivered

- Two new immutable fixture identities, `match-0c0a000000000003` and `...0004`,
  generated by `simulator.matchday_demo`. Existing original 173 fixtures and the
  two earlier mixed exports retain their bytes and hashes.
- Continuous authored player trajectories, near-goal keeper lanes, controlled
  ball aligned to its carrier, and explicit pass/shot flight paths. A majority
  of players move over 2 m in every authored 10-second interval. Position changes
  come from source observations; the renderer does not invent them.
- The saved attempt starts at its observed (82, 30) point; the later goal attempt
  starts at (88, 34) and reaches the goal line. Source confirmation still arrives
  at 104 s. Missing shot observations at 185 s remain missing, with unknown actor
  and location; the UI does not fill them in.
- Opt-in coverage catalogue defaults to the new guided discovery fixture. The
  ordinary catalogue and original reference expectations remain unchanged.
- Producer-only guided pauses stop for the first example of each family, with
  possession paused from the 60-second summary onward. They neither approve
  anything nor create source events. Turning them off allows continuous play.
  The control is local to the producer page; another controlling producer or a
  closed producer page can affect whether a client-side guided pause occurs.
- Playback controls precede the pitch. A guided review button navigates to the
  producer queue. “Show why” scrolls to/focuses the evidence and explains its
  purpose: supporting observations, not tactical causation.
- Raw observation counts live in a disclosure explaining samples versus events.

## Scope and limits

This is a scripted demonstration, not match footage or a complete football
simulation. Visible motion and physical continuity do not establish tactical
realism or usefulness. Football review, first-time viewer tasks and pilot findings
remain pending real participants. Existing reviewed explanations still require
exact-revision producer approval. Local mode makes no paid/model call.

Run `make coverage-data` to regenerate the additive catalogue, then
`make coverage-dev`. Choose the guided demo and press Play at 1×. For the full
workflow see [the walkthrough](match-coverage-demo.md). The local verification
instance uses port 3100, separately from the normal 3000 startup.

Verification and screenshots: [moving-demo results](../evals/match-coverage/moving-demo/verification.md).
